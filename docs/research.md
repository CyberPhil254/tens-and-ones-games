# Research base

What the design rests on. Each entry: the finding, what we do with it, and how strong the evidence is.

## Learning and pedagogy

| Finding | What we do | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Maths apps for young children worked best when they combined a personalised, scaffolded path with feedback that explains why an answer is right or wrong (Outhwaite et al. 2023) | Automatic levelling, a guided path, explanatory feedback on every wrong answer | Strong for app design |
| First graders who built two-digit numbers from exact tens and ones laid in a row became more accurate at placing numbers, after two 15-minute sessions (Zhang & Okamoto 2017) | Pack Ten, Puppy Walk number line with tens and ones end to end | Promising, small study |
| In number board games, counting on from the current square taught far more than counting from 1 (Laski & Siegler 2014). Number lines are a recommended core tool (IES practice guide 2021) | Race to 100: the child taps where she lands, counting on | Strong |
| Counting in tens, picking the number with a given count of tens, and reading and writing number names were the best early predictors of later multi-digit calculation (Mix et al. 2023) | Number Names game, counting labels under the tens | Strong, longitudinal |
| Finger-based training improved first graders' written addition and subtraction, still visible nine months later (Frey et al. 2024). An earlier kindergarten trial found no advantage over an active control (Schild et al. 2020) | Hands as patterns in High Five and Friends of Ten | Mixed |
| Concrete to abstract in steps (concreteness fading) helped transfer (Fyfe, McNeil & Borjas 2015), though one science study found constant concrete material did as well | Blocks, then sketch, then digits only ("in your head" levels) | Promising, mixed |
| Arithmetic fluency: time-limited practice helps, but only once children are already accurate; retrieval practice and using ten as a reference point (McNeil et al. 2025; IES 2021) | No visible timer; Quick Paws unlocks after mastery; mixed warm-ups | Strong |
| Joint parent and child use of a maths app, even weekly, raised first graders' maths achievement (Berkowitz et al. 2015) | Prompts for the adult, Race to 100 for two | Strong, RCT |
| Irrelevant appealing details lowered performance for children with weak inhibitory control | The puppy appears only as feedback; rewards are packed in tens | Moderate |
| Manipulatives and representations build understanding; fingers count as one (EEF guidance 2020) | Consistent blocks, frames, lines; fingers allowed | Guidance |

## Adaptive practice and motivation

| Finding | What we do | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Math Garden rates learners and items with an Elo-style model that scores both accuracy and response time, and serves items at about 75 percent expected success (Klinkenberg et al. 2011) | Planned engine for the app: rating per skill from accuracy and time | Strong, large scale |
| Gamification has small positive effects on learning (g about 0.49 for cognitive outcomes); a story frame mattered for behavioural outcomes (Sailer & Homner 2020) | A puppy story with progression | Moderate |
| Expected tangible rewards undermined intrinsic motivation, positive feedback enhanced it (Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999) | Rewards follow mastery; praise is specific | Strong |

## Product and legal facts (checked November 2026)

- Cambridge Primary Mathematics (0096): six stages; strands Number, Geometry and Measure, Statistics and Probability; Thinking and Working Mathematically.
- Cambridge endorsement does not cover digital tools or question banks. Do not claim endorsement or use the logo.
- Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, section 33: parental consent before processing a child's data; the regulator published a guidance note on children's data in 2025.
- Hugging Face free tier: monthly inference credits near zero (reported removed in October 2026), about five ZeroGPU minutes a day.
- Voice engines: gpt-4o-mini-tts (directable by instruction, about $0.015 per minute), ElevenLabs v3 (audio tags, most expressive, costlier), Gemini TTS (natural-language direction).
- Animation: Rive state machines for an interactive mascot; runtimes are open source and free to ship.
