# Prompts for the art

All pictures were made with Google's Nano Banana image model on 15 November 2026. Every prompt after the first was run with `source/puppy-reference.png` attached as the reference image. To add a pose or an object, reuse the matching prompt and change only the list.

## 0. Six candidate puppies

Sheet: `source/00-six-candidates.jpg`

```
A 2 x 3 grid on a pure white background, six clearly separated cells with generous white space between them. Each cell shows a DIFFERENT puppy design, candidates for a children's maths app mascot for ages 5 to 8. Put a small grey number 1 to 6 in the top left corner of each cell.

Vary between the six: breed feel (for example floppy-eared, pointy-eared, fluffy, short-haired), head and body proportions, ear and tail shape, markings, and the fur colour within warm tones.

The same in all six: full body, three-quarter view, standing, friendly and curious expression, large expressive eyes. Flat vector illustration, simple rounded shapes, bold clean outlines of even thickness, flat colours with at most one soft shadow tone, no gradients, no texture, no fur detail. Each must stay readable when shown very small on a phone.

Colours: warm fur tones only (caramel, cream, sand, golden, chocolate), dark brown nose and eyes, one small accent in sunny yellow or leaf green such as a collar. Do not use any red or blue anywhere.

No text other than the six numbers. No props, no scenery, no ground shadow.
```

Number 1 was chosen and cropped to `source/puppy-reference.png`.
